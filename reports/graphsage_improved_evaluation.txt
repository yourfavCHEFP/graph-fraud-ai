GraphSAGE Fraud Detection -- Evaluation Report (FINAL, chronological split)
Feature pipeline version: 2026-08-31-column-alignment-fix
Split strategy: chronological-transactiondt-70-15-15

Both prior known limitations are resolved in this run:
  - item 1 (feature-column alignment bug): fixed, verified against real
    data (see tests/unit/test_graph_features.py).
  - item 4 (chronological split): fixed. Real TransactionDT values from
    the raw Kaggle IEEE-CIS train_transaction.csv were mapped onto the
    graph's transaction nodes and used to build a genuine chronological
    train/val/test split (earliest 70% / next 15% / most recent 15%),
    with zero overlap and zero temporal leakage (asserted programmatically
    in src/graph/prepare_gnn_dataset.py's create_masks()).
